Enhance Your Funeral Cover with Ezweni Add-On Plans

Published on January 15, 2024 | 5 min read

At Ezweni Funerals, we believe in giving families flexible options to meet their unique needs. That's why we offer add-on plans that can be customized to provide comprehensive coverage for your specific requirements.

Understanding Add-On Plans

Add-on plans are supplementary coverage options that enhance your basic funeral plan. They allow you to tailor your coverage to include specific services, additional family members, or special arrangements that aren't covered in standard packages.

Available Add-On Options

Extended Family Coverage

Our extended family coverage allows you to include parents, siblings, or extended family members in your plan. This ensures comprehensive protection for your entire family network.

Special Ceremony Arrangements

For families with specific cultural or religious traditions, we offer add-ons for special ceremony requirements, including traditional rituals, specific music arrangements, and customized service programs.

Transportation Services

Additional transportation options include family limousines, out-of-town transportation, and repatriation services for loved ones who pass away while traveling.

Memorial Packages

Our memorial packages include tombstone installation, memorial books, online tribute pages, and annual remembrance services to honor your loved one's memory.

Benefits of Customizing Your Plan

Customizing your funeral plan with add-ons provides several key benefits:

  • Peace of mind knowing all aspects are covered
  • Financial predictability with locked-in pricing
  • Reduced stress for family members during difficult times
  • Alignment with personal and cultural preferences

Contact our planning specialists to discuss which add-on options are right for your family.
